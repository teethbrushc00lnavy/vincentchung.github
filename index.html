Research

Seeing Is Believing, but Believing Is Not Paying More: A Field Experiment on Virtual Reality and Coral Reef Valuation

Vincent Chung, Chiara Sotis, Giles Atkinson, Thomas Smith

Job Market Paper

A large field experiment in Brisbane examines whether immersive virtual reality changes environmental valuation and conservation responses by reducing psychological distance to Great Barrier Reef degradation.

Stated Preference Validity in the Age of Immersive Media: Evidence on Scope Sensitivity and Hypothetical Bias

Vincent Chung, Giles Atkinson, Thomas Smith

Working Paper

Using a preregistered 2×2 experiment (N=246), this study tests whether immersive virtual reality improves two central dimensions of stated-preference validity. Both VR and high-quality video generated scope-sensitive valuations, while neither reduced hypothetical bias.
